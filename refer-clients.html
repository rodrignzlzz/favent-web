How it works

From recommendation to commission, in four steps.

  1. You agree on the commissionOnce, with each business you partner with. It applies to every booking.
  2. Your client booksWith the QR code or NFC point at your reception, your table or your apartment, or with a link you send from the app. They pay the deposit in a minute.
  3. The deposit is heldFavent holds it with Stripe until the service is delivered. Nobody moves it before then.
  4. Your commission arrives on its ownWhen the client’s QR code is redeemed, the commission goes to your account. No invoices to chase.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

Who can recommend with Favent?

Any business with clients to recommend things to: hotels, holiday rental hosts (Airbnb and similar), restaurants and bars, agencies, concierges and other professionals in Mallorca. Favent reviews every sign-up.

How much do I earn per recommendation?

You and each business agree on the commission when you start partnering. Without a partnership, the commission the business sets on each service applies.

When and how do I get my commission?

When the service is delivered and the client’s QR code is redeemed, the commission is transferred to your payout account with Stripe.

Does the client see my commission?

No. The client only sees the price, what they pay now and what they pay on site.

What if the client cancels?

If they cancel more than 72 hours before, the deposit is refunded. If they cancel later or do not show up, the deposit is split as if the service had been delivered, and you get your commission.

Which businesses can I recommend?

Those in the Favent network in Mallorca: boats and charter, excursions, diving, cycling, transfers and private services. With Starter, those that partner with you; with Pro, the whole network.

How much does it cost?

Starter, €29 per month; Pro, €44.95 per month (+ VAT). With the founding price, the Pro plan for all of 2027 costs €99 + VAT if you send us your request before 15 February 2027 and we accept you (only 100 places).
